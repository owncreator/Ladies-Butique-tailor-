OUR JOURNEY

Dupattas and sarees play an indispensable role in the Indian tradition as a symbol of respect, with which the brand started this journey. As the journey grows, they spread their love for craftsmanship and dedication through each collection. Over the years, Begum has made its way across the globe and has also been a favourite of design icons and celebrities such as Kareena Kapoor Khan, Shilpa Shetty, Soha Ali Khan, Sonam Bajwa, and many more.
